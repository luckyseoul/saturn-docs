■Pattern name data storage location The pattern name data storage location on the scroll surface has the following restrictions, regardless of whether it is a normal scroll surface or a rotating scroll surface.
Table 3.2 shows the restrictions on VRAM mode bits and pattern name data storage locations.
There are no restrictions on the storage location of character pattern data or bitmap pattern data.

(1) When neither VRAM-A nor VRAM-B is divided into two. Storage is possible only in either VRAM-A or VRAM-B. (2) When only VRAM-A is divided into two: a) In VRAM-B. When storing, it may be stored in VRAM-A1. b) When not storing in VRAM-B, it may be stored in either VRAM-A0 or A1. (3) When only VRAM-B is divided into two a) When storing in VRAM-A, it may be stored in VRAM-B1. b) When not storing in VRAM-A, it may be stored in either VRAM-B0 or B1. (4) VRAM-A , VRAM-B are both divided into two: The data can be stored only in either VRAM-A0 or VRAM-B0, and in either VRAM-A1 or VRAM-B1.
