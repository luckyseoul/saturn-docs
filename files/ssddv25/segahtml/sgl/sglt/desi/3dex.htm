Softimage3D enhanced version
Softimage3D Extreme

Softimage3D Extreme includes Softimage3D plus advanced special effects for modeling and rendering. These special effects modules include Meta-Clay, which is based on density spheres, Mental Ray, a high-quality renderer that includes special effects, and Particles, which allows you to express particles such as fountains and explosions.

Features of Softimage3D Extreme

Meta-Clay
This is a special effects module that more realistically models the surfaces of objects with soft textures such as living organisms. Further enhances the Model module.

Custom effects (Ray Rebels)
Provides effects that enhance the expressiveness and workability of modeling. We provide a variety of motion G-effects that enable more advanced animation expressions.

Mental Ray
Equipped with numerous shader libraries as standard, achieving high quality rendering. Mental Ray is a rendering support tool that further enhances the functionality of the Matter module. In addition to pre-prepared shaders, it is also possible to create a library of shaders defined by the user, greatly expanding the variety of expressions, as well as post scripting, contour rendering, deX placement, volumetric shading, etc. , camera lens control, and a variety of other special effects. All operations are simple and can be controlled parametrically. It also supports distribution tower O/rendering, which utilizes free CPU cycles on the network to improve rendering efficiency.

Particles
Generates part numbers on a pixel basis for moving objects. Realizes detailed and realistic expressions of natural phenomena. Applying real-world dynamics simulation, you can import scene files created with Softimage3D and perform various effect settings for the scene using the part-N system. It provides a pixel x part generator to quickly generate representations of natural phenomena such as snow, fog, explosions, smoke, and fire.
