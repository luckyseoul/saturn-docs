3. sound control commands
・Command list

・SEQUENCE START----------Starts playing the song and sound effects
・SEQUENCE STOP-----------Stops playing the song and sound effects
・PAUSE-------------------Pause the song, sound effect
/PAUSE OFF---------------Cancel the pause of the song/sound effect
/SEQUENCE VOLUME---------Volume adjustment or fade in/out
・STOP ALL----------------Stop all performances
・TEMPO CHANGE------------Change the tempo of songs and sound effects
・MAP CHANGE--------------Map change
・DIRECT MIDI CONTROL-----Send MIDI events as commands
・START VOLUME ANALYZER---Start of input level analysis of Digital Audio In
・STOP VOLUME ANALYZER----End of input level analysis of Digital Audio In
・DSP STOP----------------Stop DSP , Initialization
・ALL NOTE OFF------------Stop all sounds
・SEQUENCE PAN------------Control PANPOT from the game
・SOUND INITIALYZE--------Initialize the sound driver
・YAMAHA 3D CONTROL-------YAMAHA 3D sound control
・QSOUND CONTROL----------Qsound control
・YAMAHA 3D INITIALYZE----YAMAHA 3D,Qsound Initialization of stereo location
・TEMPO MODE--------------Tempo mode setting
・TEMPO RATIO-------------Relative tempo value setting Settings
・CD-DA LEVEL-------------Adjust the level of CD-DA
・CD-DA PAN---------------CD-DA output Panpot settings
・TOTAL VOLUME------------Total volume settings
・EFFECT CHANGE-----------Effect switching
・START PCM STREAM--------Start PCM stream playback
・STOP PCM STREAM---------Stop PCM stream playback
・MIXER CHANGE------------Switch mixer
・MIXER PARAMETER CHANGE--Switch mixer parameters
・HARDWARE CHECK----------Checking the hardware
・PCM PARAMETER CHANGE----Switching parameters for the PCM stream being played
・PCM SLOT ALLOCATIONPCM--Securing a slot for the stream
・PCM SLOT RELEASEPCM-----Release stream
