Duty ratio of CD drive for Sega Saturn

Contents

Due to limitations on CD drives, please take the following precautions when developing CD-ROM software for Saturn.

◆Make sure that the duty ratio of the Saturn CD drive is within 33% for 10 minutes.

Definition: Duty ratio = [seek time/(seek time + non-seek time)]*100

Non-seek time refers to time other than seeking (playing, pausing, etc.).
