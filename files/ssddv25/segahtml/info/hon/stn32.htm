Contents

The colors and shapes of the Sega Saturn console and peripherals for overseas markets are different from the Japanese version.
Please be careful when displaying images imported from peripheral devices in applications for overseas markets. The peripheral ID and software access method are the same as the Japanese version.

■Different colors and shapes

Japan (gray) USA (black) Europe (black)
(1) standard putt Control Pad Control Pad
(2) mission stick Mission Stick Mission Stick

■Only the color is different (the shape is the same)

Japan (gray) USA (black) Europe (black)
(3) Sega Saturn/Main body SEGASATURN SEGASATURN
(4) racing controller Arcade Racer Arcade Racer
(5) shuttle mouse Segasaturn Mouse Mouse
(6) multi terminal 6Player 6Player
(7) Virchaktic Virtur Stick Virtur Stick

■Changes between Japanese version and overseas version

(1) The color is black and the shape is significantly different. Common for the US and Europe
(2) The shape of the grip, B, and C buttons will be slightly larger (same for the US and Europe)
(3)(5)(6)(7) All will be black. The US and Europe are the same
(4) The color of the start button and butterfly shift is gray, which is common in the US and Europe.

■Supplementary information

For regions outside the United States and Europe, please contact our technical support .
