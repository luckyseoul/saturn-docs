Contents

We provide Sega Saturn software creation standards.

What are software creation standards?
Software Creation Standards is a document that describes uniform standards for Sega Saturn brand games.
Please be sure to understand the contents before creating your game.
Please also note that we will not be able to release a game that does not comply with the strict rules.

Type of creation criteria
Two types are available: one for SEGA brand and one for licensee brand.
We will distribute one copy at our discretion, but if you wish to receive the other copy, please contact our office or technical support .
