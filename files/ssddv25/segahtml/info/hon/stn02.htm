STN-2

Do not use pre-emphasis

 STN-2

 Subject supplement:

Contents

The use of pre-emphasis is prohibited regardless of the use of SCSP.

reason
On the Saturn, emphasis is controlled by the final stage DAC, so if you play a pre-emphasized CD-DA, de-emphasis will also be applied to the sound output from the SCSP.

that's all
