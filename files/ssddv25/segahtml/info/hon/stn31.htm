Contents

The corresponding area symbols and area symbols will be changed as follows, depending on the Sega Saturn shipping region.

＜ Table 1. Corresponding area symbols and area symbols＞
Shipping area South Korea Brazil Asia PAL Latin America PAL
old symbol 6H・"K" 5H・"B" AH・"A" DH・"L"
New symbol 2H・"T" 4H・"U" CH・"E" CH・"E"

■Response area symbol and area symbol to be used

“J” Domestic NTSC Japan
“T” Asia NTSC Asia region (Taiwan, Philippines, South Korea)
“U” North America NTSC North America region (USA, Canada), Central and South America (Brazil only)
“E” PAL Europe region, Southeast Asia (China, Middle East), Central and South America

■Notes

Please note that the following compatible area codes and area symbols cannot be used.

●Corresponding area code to be deleted
"K", "B", "A", "L"

●Area symbol that becomes “SEGA RESEVED”
05H, 06H, 0AH, 0DH
