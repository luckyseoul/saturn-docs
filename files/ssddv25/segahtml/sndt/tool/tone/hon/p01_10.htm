Tone Editor User's Manual

1. Introduction

SEGA ToneEditor is upwardly compatible with YAMAHA ToneEditor in terms of edit data and full compatibility in output data. The differences with YAMAHA ToneEditor are listed below.

  1. Improved maintainability.
  2. Display of layers and voices that exist in different trees at the same time.
  3. Compact layer data window.
  4. Reducing the number of windows.
  5. Introduction of project management methods.
  6. Editing multiple data
