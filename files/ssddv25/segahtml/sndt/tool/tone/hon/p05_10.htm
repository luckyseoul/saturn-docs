Tone Editor User's Manual

5. menu

Here we will provide an overview of the menu bar that makes up ToneEditor and its members.

◆Apple menu

◆File menu
Manages input/output of all files and terminates applications.

◆Edit menu
You can cut and paste voices and layers, insert and delete banks, replace and delete internal waveforms, etc.

◆Settings menu
Issues host commands to change mixer, velocity, PEG, and PLFO synthesizer parameters and to change master volume (DirectVolume).

◆Function menu
Configure transmission-related settings and initial settings.
