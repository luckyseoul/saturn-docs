Sega Saturn Software Creation Standards/Sega Brand Version/Ver3.00

3. sega logo

1) Obligation to display the Sega logo

  • "License SEGA logo" immediately after powering on
    Data provided by SEGA must be displayed without modification.

  • "SEGA logo" in the title loop
    Be sure to display it in accordance with the regulations in “3-3) Screen display.”
    However, this does not apply if there is a license agreement with another company and the license indication is separately stipulated in the agreement (be sure to check the agreement and follow it).

    *Regarding the logo display of the outsourcing company
    Be sure to ask your superiors for instructions, and if it is determined that it will be displayed, it will be inserted after the "SEGA logo" display in the title loop.

2) Skip Sega logo
Immediately after the power is turned on (or when the ``reset button on the main unit'' is pressed),

  1. “SEGASATURN logo” displayed in the main body BOOT ROM

  2. "License SEGA logo" displayed when starting up

  3. "SEGA logo" display within the application

2.Skip the "SEGA logo" in the application immediately after the "License SEGA logo" is displayed at startup (the first "SEGA logo" (Skip the Sega logo).
When displaying the logo of an outsourcing company, the display is also skipped in the same way.

3) Screen display
Display the registered trademark [SEGATM] in the center of the TV/monitor screen. Please use the version with [TM] for both domestic and overseas versions.
Since it is a registered trademark, please be sure to use the same shape as our registered logo. For details, please refer to the "SEGA Enterprises Visual Identity Manual" published by the SEGA General Affairs Department. There are no restrictions on size.

4) Obligation to display final form
The Sega logo will always be displayed in its final form (registered trademark form) for approximately 2 seconds.
Even if the start button is pressed before the final form is displayed, the final form will always be displayed once before proceeding to the next screen. (It must not be possible to proceed to the next screen with a different shape.)
