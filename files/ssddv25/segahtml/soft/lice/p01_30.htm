3. Company name logo

1) Obligation to display the license Sega logo
Immediately after the power is turned on, the "License Sega Logo" always displays the data provided by Sega without changing it.

2) Company name logo display
Display of the licensee company logo is optional. Please refer to the following Sega company regulations when displaying the Sega logo.

Reference) Sega company regulations when displaying the Sega logo
Display the registered trademark [SEGATM] in the center of the TV/monitor screen. Please use the version with [TM] for both domestic and overseas versions.
Since it is a registered trademark, please be sure to use the same shape as our registered logo. For details, please refer to the "SEGA Enterprises Visual Identity Manual" published by the SEGA General Affairs Department. There are no restrictions on size.

The Sega logo will always be displayed in its final form (registered trademark form) for approximately 2 seconds.
Even if the start button is pressed before the final form is displayed, the final form will always be displayed once before proceeding to the next screen. (It must not be possible to proceed to the next screen with a different shape.)
