Double dense interlace enable bit: double interlace enable (DIE), bit 3

Double interlace plot line: double interlace plot line (DIL), bit 2
  • Single density interlace displays the same picture in even and odd fields. On the other hand, double-dense interlace increases the vertical resolution by a factor of two by displaying different pictures (even and odd lines only, respectively) in even and odd fields.
  • DIE = 1 enables double-dense interlace.
  • The contents of the first display screen after changing the DIE are not guaranteed.
  • In double-dense interlace, set FCM = FCT = 0 (1 cycle mode) to switch the field every 1/60 seconds.
