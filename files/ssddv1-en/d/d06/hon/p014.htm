● Store 

> opt Destination (s) 

opt: Store option. Any one of {S1 / S2 / S3} or omitted.
Destination (s): Specify the store destination. Select a total of 1 or more from “a group to d group” shown below. Cannot be omitted. 

 a group 
 One of {TEMP00 to 7F} 
 b group 
 FREG 
 c group 
 ADREG 
 d group 
 One of {EFREG00 ~ 0F} 

  Note  When ADREG is specified for Destination (s), S3 must be specified for opt

  • opt specifies the bit shift, overflow protection, and store mode of the data to be stored. The combinations are shown in the table below.
