Multiply addition

@ pM * pC ± (pM * pC ± (... (pM * pC ± (pM * pC + pA)) ...)))

pM: Multiplier
pC: Multiplication coefficient
pA: Number of additions

* The number of parentheses "(" and ")" must match.
* The rightmost "(" must be on the left side of the leftmost ")".
