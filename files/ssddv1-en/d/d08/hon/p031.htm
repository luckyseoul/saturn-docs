Scroll

Scroll commands that operate on the active text window.
The letters displayed at the back of the menu indicate the currently set shortcut keys.

One line roll down: Scrolls down one line.

One line rollup: Scrolls up one line.

Half screen roll down: Scrolls down half the screen.

Half-screen rollup: Scrolls up half the screen.
