MIDI channel and voice

The
Saturn sound system can handle up to 32 channels (tracks) of MIDI data at the same time, so if MIDI 1 channel is used as one instrument, it can handle up to 32 instruments simultaneously. However, in other words, you have to play all the songs and sound effects in this. There is no problem in situations where songs and sound effects are sounded independently, but if songs and sound effects, songs and songs, and sound effects and sound effects are pronounced at the same time, they must be assigned to different channels. In this system, 32 MIDI channels are dynamically assigned (DVA) to produce sound.
Therefore, each sequence data must have this information at the beginning of the track. In order to play with the correct tone, be sure to specify the bank (Control Change Bank Select) and voice (Program Change) for each track of the sequence data.
