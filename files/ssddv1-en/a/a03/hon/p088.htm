Access procedure in SH-2 direct mode for peripherals with megadrive peripheral IDs other than 5H

Access procedure in SH2 direct mode for peripherals with mega drive peripheral IDs other than 5H is shown below.

  1. Get Mega Drive Peripheral ID.
  2. Determine peripheral by Megadrive peripheral ID. Data is acquired by executing a sequence corresponding to it. Peripherals with megadrive peripheral IDs other than 5H do not return a Saturn peripheral ID.
