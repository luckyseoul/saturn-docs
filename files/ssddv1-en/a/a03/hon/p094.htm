■ Sega Tap

SEGATAP is a 3-wire handshake peripheral. Sega Tap has two types of sequences: power-on reset and data request.

  1. Power-on reset
    When the power is turned on, the tap will automatically initialize. SEGATAP sets the TL bit to 0B during initialization and informs SH-2 that initialization is in progress.
    Do not make a data request until initialization is complete.
