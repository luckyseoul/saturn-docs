SCU(System Control Unit)
Equipped with a CPU I / F, A-Bus I / F, and B-Bus I / F controller to smoothly transfer data between each bus. In addition, a DMA controller, interrupt controller, and DSP are installed inside to realize DMA control, interrupt control, and high-speed arithmetic processing.

Main CPU
Equipped with a 32-bit RISC CPU SH-2 that controls the entire system.

MC68EC000
Sound control CPU centered on SCSP. It plays the control function of SCSP.

V-BLANK-IN
One of three types of blanking interrupts. This interrupt indicates the end timing of screen display.

V-BLANK-OUT
One of three types of blanking interrupts. This interrupt indicates the screen display start timing.

H-BLANK-IN
One of three types of blanking interrupts. This interrupt indicates the display end timing of one line.

Peripheral
Peripheral devices connected to Saturn, such as control PAD, mouse, keyboard, etc.
