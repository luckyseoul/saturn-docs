The slot-specific control register indicates the register allocation configured in each of the 32 slots (SLOT0 to SLOT31).
∙ The bit allocation position of each register is expressed as [3: 0]. For example, SA [19:16] indicates that the 16th to 19th bits of SA are allocated.
