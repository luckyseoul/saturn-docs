Effect data
Indicates the sound obtained as a result of applying the effect to the sound generated by the sound source section through DSP.

Reverb
A type of sound field effect that can create an atmosphere like a hall, stage room, or iron plate.

PLL(Phase Locked Loop)
A phase synchronization circuit (IC) that follows the phase of the input signal.

IPL(Initial Program Loading)
One of the list processing languages. The instruction to load the program is output from the specified I / O device to the processing unit.

Clipping
The removal of image data outside the specified drawing access area.

PAL (Phase Alternation by Line system)
A standard system for color television broadcasting developed in West Germany with 625 scanning lines and 25 images per second.

NTSC (National Television System Committee system)
Refers to the standard format for color television broadcasting with 525 scanning lines and 30 images per second, approved by the FCC (Federal Communications Commission) and adopted in Japan, the United States, Korea, and other countries.
