4.6 Notes on adding / changing settings during CD playback

In the stream system, settings such as stream keys can be changed dynamically during CD playback. However, after requesting processing for the CD block, there is a certain delay before the setting contents become valid.
For this reason, the following functions must be issued at least 10 sectors before the target position.
