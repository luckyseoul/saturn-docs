7.2 68000 error status bitmap details 

 bit position 
 error details 
bit31
68K CPU address error
bit30
68K CPU bass error
 bit29 
 68K CPU exception handling 
bit28-bit24
reserved
 bit23 
 The specified DSP RAM area is invalid during Effect change. 
 bit22 
 Effect change number is invalid. 
bit21-bit17
reserved
 bit16 
 There is no layer that should be pronounced by Key Sprit when executing Note-on. 
bit15
reserved
 bit14 
 Undefined MIDI Control change Number input 
 bit13 
 At the time of Effect change, the corresponding DSP RAM area is insufficient. 
 bit12 
 During RAM change, the DSP RAM setting value is incorrect. 
 bit11 
 During the effect change, the DSP RAM does not exist in the map. 
 bit10 
 At the time of Effect change, the DSP program is not downloaded. 
 bit9 
 At the time of Effect change, the corresponding DSP Program does not exist in the map. 
 bit8 
 At the start of the sequence, the corresponding sequence bank has not been downloaded. 
 bit7 
 When the sequence starts, the corresponding sequence song number does not exist in the sequence bank. 
 bit6 
 When the sequence starts, the corresponding sequence bank does not exist in the map. 
 bit5 
 During MIDI Prg change, the corresponding program number does not exist in the tone bank. 
 bit4 
 At the time of MIDI Prg change, the tone number bank of the corresponding management number & MIDI channel is not set. 
 bit3 
 When MIDI Control change (mixer change), the corresponding control number & MIDI channel tone bank is set 
 Not. 
 bit2 
 There is no corresponding tone bank in the map at the time of Mixer change or tone bank change. 
 Or the sound bank is not downloaded. 
 bit1 
 At the time of Map change, the corresponding map does not exist in (A000h ~). 
 bit0 
 Mixer change does not exist in the tone bank. Or the tone bank is not set. 

 
      Note: Bits 24-31 are not automatically cleared. Clear them on the host side if necessary.
Bits 0-23 are automatically cleared at the next normal operation.
