Chapter 2 Sound Driver Startup

2.1 Sound driver startup method

    To start up the sound driver, basically just transfer the sound driver to the sound memory and cancel the reset signal. However, the memory contents at power-on are not guaranteed, so initialization such as zero clear is required.

    To start the sound driver, follow steps 1 to 6 below. This starts the sound driver and starts from the beginning of the program. For details on hardware such as SCSP and reset, refer to the SCSP and SMPC user's manuals.
