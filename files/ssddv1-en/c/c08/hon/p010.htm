    CD-DA playback 
     How to pronounce 
     This is a method to directly play back the audio recorded on the audio track of the CD. 
     It's exactly the same way as a commercially available music CD. 
     Features 
     This is a method to play back the long waveform data recorded in advance. 
     Audio is automatically output by the hardware. 
     Advantages 
    -Since live recording is possible, you can play real and highly expressive performances. 
    -No load on the main and sound CPU. 
     Disadvantages 
    -It is not easy to change or modify songs. You have to record again. 
    -A large amount of waveform data is required with a data width of 16 bits and a fixed frequency of 44.1KHz. 
    -CD access is always required during playback, and the CD drive will be occupied. 
    -The tempo cannot be changed in real time.
