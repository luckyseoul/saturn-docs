4.2 Menu screen function details

If you press
    [SPACE], "Menu" on the top bar will be displayed in white letters.

    You can select "Menu" or "Help" with [LEFT] and [RIGHT] cursors. The selected part is displayed in white letters.
    When you press [ENTER] or [DOWN] on the cursor, the contents of the selected menu will appear.


    Select a menu item with [UP] or [DOWN] and press [ENTER] or [SPACE] to perform processing according to that menu item.
