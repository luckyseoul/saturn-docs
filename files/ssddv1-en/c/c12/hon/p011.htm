■ How to exit 

  [Procedure 9] Press [SPACE] on the PC compatible machine on which the virtual CD is running.

      [Result] "Menu" on the top bar is highlighted.

      "Menu" or "Help" is alternately highlighted by [LEFT] and [RIGHT] of the cursor, but make sure that Menu is highlighted. 

  [Procedure 10] Press [ENTER] or the [DOWN] key of the cursor. 

 [Procedure 11] Use the [DOWN] key to highlight the last menu item Exit. 

 [Procedure 12] Press the [ENTER] key.

      This will exit the virtual CD emulator.
