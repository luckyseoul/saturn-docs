(3) Playback parameter setting method

        For playback parameters, specify the playback range and playback mode. The playback range is specified by a combination of position parameters for the start and end positions. 
         (a) Track specification and frame address specification cannot be combined with the playback range.
In that case, REJECT is returned. All other playback range combinations are possible.
(B) All combinations of playback range and playback mode are possible.
(C) The playback range and the maximum number of repeats are retained in the CD block and are valid until reset.
(D) Default value: Disc start to end, no repeat, move pickup

(4) Exceptions to playback range

        If the end position is less than the start position, it will not be played. Just like outside the playback range, it will be in the 〈PAUSE〉 state.
        (However, the playback range is retained and the playback mode is also valid.
