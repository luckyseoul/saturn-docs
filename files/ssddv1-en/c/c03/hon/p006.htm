2. DISC physical format

Physical format conforms to CD-ROM standard (YELLOW BOOK) and CD-ROM XA standard.
This section describes the points that SEGA specifically limits within this standard. It also describes the matters necessary for developing an application.

2.1 Area division on disk and arrangement of each area

The disk is divided and arranged from the inner circumference in the following order. 
     (1) Lead-in area
(2) Program area
(3) Lead-out area
TOC information is written in the lead-in area according to the “CD-ROM XA standard”.

Figure 2.1 Arrangement of each area on the disc

Only one track is required for the CD-ROM area (Mode 1).
The CD-DA area must have at least one track.
