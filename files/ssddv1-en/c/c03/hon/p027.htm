    Version (start address: 2AH) 
       definition Enter the version of the application.
Allowed characters Uppercase “V”, numbers, “.” (Period)
Number of characters: 6 characters
Entry rules: Always start with “V”, then one digit, then “.”, Then three digits.
The final release will be V1.000.
Entry example: Sample disc → V0.801
For master disk → V1.000

    Release date (Start address: 30H) 

       definition Enter the date when the master disk (write-once disk) was created.
Usable characters: numbers only
Number of characters: 8 characters
Entry rules: 4 digits for the year and 2 digits for the date.
Entry example 19940912 (September 12, 1994)

    Device information (start address: 38H) 

       definition Enter the device information. In the case of a CD, enter the number of sheets in the set.
Available characters Alphanumeric and “/-”
Number of characters: 8 characters
Filling rules Fill the empty space with a space.
Entry example: 1 CD set, 1 CD → CD-1 / 1 △△
, CD 3-disc set, 2nd case → CD-2 / 3 △△

    Supported area symbol (start address: 40H) 

       definition Enter the area symbol of the region where you want to run the application.
Available characters Only uppercase alphabetic characters specified in the area symbol list below
Number of characters 10 characters
Entry rules Multiple entries are allowed. Fill the area symbol and area symbol together, with spaces or spaces between them.
* Do not include a comma. Fill the space with a space.
● Area symbol list
J Japan Japan: J
Asia NTSC (Taiwan, Philippines, Korea): T
North America region (USA, Canada) Latin America NTSC (Brazil): U
Europe PAL, East Asia PAL, Latin America PAL: E
