1.7 ISO9660 compliant

In the ISO9660 standard, the contents of logical sector numbers 0 to 15 are not specified. CD producers can use it for boot programs and protection information for system startup. By specifying this area in the disk format, GAME-CD can be started. No startup cartridge is required. The logical sector number 16 and later conforms to ISO9660.
