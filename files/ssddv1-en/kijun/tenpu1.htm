Taking into account the fact that it is a consumer game that can be easily distributed to ordinary households, be careful not to incorporate into the game anything that is unethical or educational. Although the specific contents are not mentioned here, follow the provisions set forth in the “Guidelines for Ethics Rules for Television Game Software” for rating examination.

Pay attention to the following points so as not to infringe on the intellectual property rights and intellectual property rights of third parties in all graphics, sounds, software, and projects used in the game.

2. Specific examples of trademarks

a) Prohibition of use of “RPG”

Strictly observe the following decisions regarding the use of words such as “RPG” in the June 29, 1992 Circular from the Legal Department and the July 6, 1992 minutes.

  • Do not use “RPG” in Japan or overseas

    Japan: Cannot be used because Bandai owns the registered trademark
    United States: Not available because IBM has a registered trademark
    Europe: Not available due to registered trademarks and registered similar trademarks

  • The Kana character “Role Playing Game” is displayed as a general noun in the sentence, such as “This is a role playing game.”
  • The English letter “Role Playing Game” should not be used in Japan.
  • Kana characters such as “low play game” are used as an alternative to “RPG”
