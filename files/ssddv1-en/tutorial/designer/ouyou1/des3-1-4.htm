Roof modification

Use [Trans] in the TAG mode to correct the roof to the position and shape of an automobile.
First, let's make the points in the figure TAG (Figure 3-8).

Figure 3-8 Attaching a TAG in front of the bottom of the roof

Set the

mode to TAG mode and select the [TransZ] icon to move in the + Z direction (the degree of movement is such that the Z value of the point is about 1.0 m). Please adjust it by looking at the Right window.) Then remove the TAG.

Next, set the point in the figure to TAG (Figure 3-9).

Figure 3-9 Attaching a TAG to the rear of the lower part of the roof

Select the

[TransZ] icon and move it in the -Z direction. (The degree of movement is the right window so that the Z value of the point is about -0.85 m.) Please adjust it by watching). When finished, remove the TAG (Figure 3-10).

Figure 3-10 After deformation of the lower part of the roof

In addition, set the two points shown in the figure below to TAG (Figure 3-11).

Figure 3-11 Attaching a TAG in front of the top of the roof

Click the

[TransZ] icon to move in the -Z direction (Figure 3-12). (The degree of movement should be adjusted in the right window so that the Z value of the point is about 0.5 m.)

Figure 3-12 After deformation of the top of the roof

Finally, shape the roof. Set the four points in the figure to TAG (Figure 3-13).

Figure 3-13 Attaching TAG to the top of the roof

Check that you are in TAG mode, click the [ScaleX] icon, and move the mouse while pressing the button to reduce the distance between the points in the X direction. .

The result is shown in the figure (Figure 3-14).

Fig. 3-14 After roof top deformation
